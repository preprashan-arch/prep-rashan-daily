Privacy Policy – Prep-Rashan Uploads

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Prep-Rashan Uploads is a private publishing tool used only by Prep-Rashan to upload its own educational videos to its own YouTube channel (Prep-Rashan Tutorial). It is not offered to the public.

What the app accesses

With the channel owner's permission, the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, set their titles, descriptions and thumbnails, and read the channel's own basic details and statistics.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, store or process any personal data of viewers or other YouTube users.
  • We do not sell or share any data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • Access tokens are held only by the publishing service used to post the videos and are used only for the purposes above.

YouTube

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at Google account permissions.
